Field note / trust

Personal by design.

What stays here

Saved places, visited places, filters, and owner edits are stored only in this browser. Dario’s List does not require an account and does not include advertising trackers.

What comes from providers

When a connected place detail or live map is opened, the browser may request current place facts, photos, or map tiles from Google. Those live facts are displayed for the session and are not written into Dario’s List storage.

What the list owns

Dario’s notes, tags, groupings, and neighborhood indexes are the editorial layer. Provider facts can change, so hours, closures, and directions should be confirmed before making a trip.
